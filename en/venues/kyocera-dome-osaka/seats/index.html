Kyocera Dome Osaka seat finder

Choose the Area, enter the row and seat number, and the spot calculated from the official seating chart is marked.

An unofficial guide. The stage position and the seats in use change from show to show.
Check your final seat on your ticket and with the venue's guidance on the day.
The map is a sketch drawn from the blocks of the official seating chart. Seat positions are estimates that assume 20 seats in a row of each block (on the official chart, neighboring block numbers go up by 20). Blocks A to L behind home plate are Center; the rest are the 3rd base side or the 1st base side. This map can find seats in 288 of the 376 blocks; seats in the other blocks are not found.
