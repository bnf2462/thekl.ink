Summary

A Sports Kyunghyang headline on 2023/1/23 called her "the second Lisa has arrived" - BABYMONSTER's youngest member and the one with the shortest trainee period (1 year 9 months). A singer from Thailand, she debuted with "BATTER UP" in 2023 as one of the original six BABYMONSTER members.[2][3]
