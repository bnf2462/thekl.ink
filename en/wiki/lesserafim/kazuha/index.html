Summary

A former ballerina who studied ballet from age 3 and trained at the Dutch National Ballet Academy. A singer and dancer from Japan. A member of LE SSERAFIM under SOURCE MUSIC. Debuted as a member of LE SSERAFIM in 2022.[2][3][1]
