Summary

She debuted in two groups, IZ*ONE and LE SSERAFIM, and leads LE SSERAFIM. A singer from South Korea. A member and leader of LE SSERAFIM under SOURCE MUSIC. She debuted as a member of IZ*ONE in 2018 via Mnet's “PRODUCE 48”, and re-debuted as the leader of LE SSERAFIM in 2022.[4][1][2]
