Summary

A Korean singer-dancer who was scouted by SM Entertainment at a dance festival in Yangsan in her third year of middle school, and debuted in 2020 as the first publicly revealed member of aespa. A member of aespa under SM Entertainment, and also a member of SM's female supergroup GOT the beat since 2022.[1][2]

Moments / Memes

She became a topic of discussion for being "cute" even before her debut, and is called by dozens of nicknames by members, staff, and fans. The one she likes best is "Mindungi."[5][3]

  • The official Short's title itself calls her "#金ポピ," and the nickname — given because she's "adorable from head to toe" — stuck. Karina had called her this first in a regram, and the official account later endorsed it too.

  • In an early-release clip ahead of the main broadcast of Weekly Idol EP.513, she solo-performed Ottokke Song, a regular idol segment of appealing for her own song in a cutesy voice. This is where the nickname Kim Ottokke took root.

    • 2021/5/25
