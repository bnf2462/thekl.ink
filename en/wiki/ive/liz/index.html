Summary

Born in 2004 on Jeju Island. In the summer of 2019, as a 3rd-year middle school student, she joined Starship Entertainment through a Pepsi summer public audition, transferred to a middle school in Seoul, and spent her trainee years there. She debuted as a member of IVE in 2021. Her stage name “LIZ” comes from the nickname of Elizabeth Bennet, the heroine of British author Jane Austen's “Pride and Prejudice”. Along with An Yujin, she is considered the group's de facto main vocalist. Signed to Starship Entertainment.[2]
