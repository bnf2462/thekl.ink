Age 14In 2010 she was the only one to pass YG Entertainment's audition in Thailand, and spent five years and three months as a trainee in Korea from 2011/4. Told to live in Korean only to debut in Korea, she studied Korean one-on-one with a teacher for two hours every day before her debut.NL
