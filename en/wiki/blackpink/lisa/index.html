History

1.Early Life

BirthBorn on 1997/3/27 in Buriram, Thailand, she grew up in Bangkok from the age of three. She attended Praphamontree II International School, a private school with many classes in English. From kindergarten she went to dance school and competed with the dance crew “We Zaa Cool”.[4]

Age 14In 2010 she was the only one to pass YG Entertainment's audition in Thailand, and spent five years and three months as a trainee in Korea from 2011/4. Told to live in Korean only to debut in Korea, she studied Korean one-on-one with a teacher for two hours every day before her debut.[4]
