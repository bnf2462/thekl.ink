History

1.Early Life

BirthBorn on 1995/1/03 in Sanbon-dong, Gunpo-si, Gyeonggi-do. After graduating from Taeeul Elementary School she entered Yongho Middle School, then moved to Gwacheon, transferred to Gwacheon Middle School and graduated there.NJ

Age 16At Gwacheon Jungang High School she joined the drama club aiming to become an actor, and in the summer vacation of her second year she applied to actors' agency auditions. Her first YG Entertainment audition was as an actor, but after a meeting with the head of the company she joined as a trainee for a girl group. At the audition she sang 이은미's “애인있어요”. She spent about five years as a trainee from 2011/7 and left high school in the second term of her second year (she later passed the high school equivalency exam).NJ
