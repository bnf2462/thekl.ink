1.Early Life

BirthBorn on 1996/1/16 in Bundang-gu, Seongnam-si, Gyeonggi-do. From third grade at Cheongdam Elementary School in Seoul, she spent about five years studying in Auckland, New Zealand to learn English.P,K

Age 8It started with a family trip when she was 8. Her mother asked whether she would like to live there; she said yes, and they went the following year. She cried on the day she arrived, but from the next day she settled in quickly. She has said that New Zealand's education, which values deciding things for yourself, broadened the way she sees things.Kt0 なし

Age 10She appeared in an MBC special documentary on English, aired 2006/6/07, as a young student studying in New Zealand.R年齢は誕生日 1996/1/16 と放送日 2006/6/07 から数えた

Age 14Around the age of 14, her mother was thinking of moving her to a school in Florida, USA. On the trip to look around, she confided that rather than live alone abroad she wanted to work in music, and she decided to return to South Korea.Kt0 なし

She had loved K-POP since her days in New Zealand, and thought that if she went into entertainment she wanted to join YG. She came back in her second year of middle school (age 14), passed YG's audition, and spent 5 years and 11 months as a trainee from 2010/8.K,I

As a new trainee she often practiced with covers of Western songs that had rap in them. She has said on a radio show in the USA that she was given the rap because no other trainee could speak English.K,SB

Age 16On 2012/8/30, YG released a video of her as a trainee. Its introduction gives her age as 16 and her languages as Korean, English and Japanese. On 2013/1/21, a video of her singing and rapping followed as “YG NEW ARTISTS”.U
