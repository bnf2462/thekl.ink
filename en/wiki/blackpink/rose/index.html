History

1.Early Life

BirthBorn on 1997/2/11 in New Zealand. At seven she moved with her family to Melbourne, Australia, where she grew up (her parents are Korean).[5]

Age 15She entered YG Entertainment's global audition in Sydney, which her father had seen in the news, and passed in first place (she sang Jason Mraz's “I Won't Give Up”). She left Canterbury Girls' Secondary College and spent about four years and two months as a trainee from 2012/5.[5]
